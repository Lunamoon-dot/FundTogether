# SIM-02 Part K — external source memo

**Access date:** 2026-10-28  
**Scope:** bounded primary-source audit only. This memo records what the cited sources describe and the evidentiary limits of transferring those descriptions to a separate study. It does not validate, reproduce, or make a claim about FundTogether, its simulator, or any SIM-02 result.

| Source | What the source establishes | What it does not establish for FundTogether |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Kleros Court](https://docs.kleros.io/products/court) and [Kleros “What happens during a dispute?”](https://docs.kleros.io/products/court/what-happens-during-a-dispute) (official documentation) | Kleros describes a dispute-resolution protocol with randomly drawn jurors, evidence, optional hashed-vote commit, voting, appeal, and execution stages. The court is selected by the integrating dapp and may be specialized by subcourt. | It does not establish that any other system is a faithful Kleros implementation, that a three-voter majority has Kleros-equivalent security, or that Kleros-style incentives solve truth, Sybil, collusion, liveness, or evidence-quality problems in another design. |
| [Kleros FAQ](https://docs.kleros.io/kleros-faq) (official documentation) | Kleros states that jurors self-select into subcourts, stake affects selection, non-response can be penalized, and the system does not ensure jurors actually reviewed evidence; economic coherence incentives are the stated mitigation. | It does not provide empirical parameter values, error rates, or attack-resistance estimates transferable to FundTogether, nor does it establish that majority coherence equals factual correctness. |
| [UMA: How does UMA’s oracle work?](https://github.com/UMAprotocol/uma-docs/blob/master/protocol-overview/how-does-umas-oracle-work.md) and [OptimisticOracleV3.sol](https://github.com/UMAprotocol/protocol/blob/master/packages/core/contracts/optimistic-oracle-v3/implementation/OptimisticOracleV3.sol) (official documentation and source) | UMA documents an optimistic flow: a proposer's bonded assertion is accepted after a challenge period if undisputed; a dispute posts a bond and escalates to an oracle/DVM or configured escalation manager. The contract source specifies assertion, liveness, bond, dispute, settlement, and callback state. | It does not establish that optimistic acceptance is suitable for a particular evidence or milestone question, that a chosen bond/liveness is adequate, or that UMA’s DVM/escalation semantics apply to another oracle, jury, or binary outcome model. |
| [Reality.eth documentation](https://realitio.github.io/docs/html/index.html), [arbitrators](https://realitio.github.io/docs/html/arbitrators.html), and [white paper](https://reality.eth.link/app/docs/html/whitepaper.html) (official documentation) | Reality.eth describes crowd-sourced questions, bonded answers, answer takeover by increasing the bond, timeout-based settlement, and optional arbitration. Its white paper explicitly places truth resolution beyond the base mechanism and passes that problem to an arbitrator chosen by the integration. | It does not establish that bond escalation yields truth, that a selected arbitrator is decentralized or unbiased, or that Reality.eth’s natural-language question process maps to any particular study outcome or protocol state. |
| [Efron, “Bootstrap Methods: Another Look at the Jackknife”](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176344552) (Institute of Mathematical Statistics, *Annals of Statistics*, 1979) | This primary paper introduces the bootstrap as replacing an unknown distribution with the empirical distribution to estimate sampling variability and construct confidence intervals, subject to the data/statistic and resampling scheme. | It does not choose the correct resampling unit, dependence structure, interval method, estimand, or stopping rule for a paired binary simulation. It does not justify a superiority, equivalence, or non-inferiority conclusion by itself. |
| [FDA, Non-Inferiority Clinical Trials](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/non-inferiority-clinical-trials) and [final guidance PDF](https://www.fda.gov/media/78504/download?attachment=) (official agency guidance) | The guidance explains that interpretable non-inferiority designs require a justified margin, appropriate design/analysis, and confidence-interval-based testing; it warns that an unjustified margin can produce a false effectiveness conclusion. | It is drug/biologic regulatory guidance, not a protocol for a simulation study. It does not supply a margin, paired-binary test, bootstrap method, sample size, or decision threshold for FundTogether or SIM-02. |

## Audit boundary

These sources are mechanism and statistical-method references, not evidence that the cited systems share the same actors, sampling frame, incentives, dependence, outcome definitions, or failure modes as any other system. Any use in SIM-02 would require an independently frozen mapping from source-described concepts to study variables; this memo makes no such mapping and reports no simulation output.
